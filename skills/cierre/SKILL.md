name: cierre
description: Closing protocol. Run when Vicente says goodbye, wants to close the conversation, or asks to wrap up the session.
---

Run these in order, show what you wrote in each, and keep the final summary short.
Skip a step that has nothing new, and say you skipped it.

1. **Logbook** → `~/Mother/bitacora/<YYYY-MM>.md`: date, project, what was done, what
   came out (with numbers), state, and the concrete next step.
2. **Documentation** → `~/Mother/docs/` and `INDEX.md`: what we explored and learned,
   with sources; update project state and "Open". If `INDEX.md` passes ~100 lines,
   move detail to `docs/`.
3. **Decisions and discards** → `decisiones.md`, each discard with "what would reopen it".
4. **How I worked** → the "What I have learned" section of `Mother.md`: corrections
   received, rules not applied, approaches confirmed. A tendency with three pieces of
   evidence becomes a firm rule. Say what you changed.
5. **Personal material** noticed this session, if any: tell Vicente.
6. **Commit** in `~/Mother/`, following core commit conventions, and report what went in.
   Push — Vicente has standing authorization to push this repo (multi-machine workflow).

End with: what is done, what is pending, the next step.
